# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

This page explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when you use Density and its website. The exact information processed depends on the features and external services you choose to use.

## 1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use Density, information you choose to enter may include project files, prompts, code, provider API keys, account details, and support messages.

## 2. Local project data

Project files and related content may be accessed on your device when you use local project features. You should review Android permissions and only grant access that is needed for the features you use.

## 3. AI providers and integrations

If you configure an AI provider or another integration, the prompts, code, and other information you submit may be sent to that provider to process your request. That provider handles the information under its own privacy policy and terms. Check the provider's policies before sending confidential or personal information.

## 4. Accounts and licensing

If account, authentication, or licensing features are enabled, relevant account or license information may be processed by the services used to provide those features. Authentication may involve third-party services such as GitHub.

## 5. Website and hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as request and server logs, when visitors access the website. Refer to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for details.

## 6. Sharing

Density does not sell your personal information. Information may be handled by third-party services you choose to connect, or where disclosure is required by law. Avoid sharing secrets, credentials, or sensitive data in prompts or support requests.

## 7. Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect information, but no software, device, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep API keys private and use the security controls provided by connected services.

## 8. Your choices

You can choose whether to configure third-party providers and what information to submit to them. You can stop using integrations and remove locally stored information through the relevant app or device controls, where available.

## 9. Children

Density is not designed to encourage children to share personal information. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, contact us.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Density changes. The current version and its update date will be shown on this page.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact [densityapp@proton.me](mailto:densityapp@proton.me).
